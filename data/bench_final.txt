ASSUMPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * 'snapshot' reimplements the live-traffic approach of mainstream
    navigation apps. It is NOT Google Maps or Waze itself, and no
    claim about their real-world performance is made here.
  * Baseline plans on data lagged by 300 s, modelling floating-car GPS
    aggregation latency. Triffy uses camera data at zero lag.
  * All routers are scored by driving their chosen route through the
    same simulated world, never against their own estimate.
  * Triffy's historical model carries a per-edge bias, so it does
    not secretly know the simulator's ground truth.
